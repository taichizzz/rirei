# Asset Provenance

## Rirei Application Icon

`AppIcon.icns` was generated with Google Gemini in 2026 under the direction of taichizzz for
the Rirei project. taichizzz selected and adapted the output for use as the application icon
and makes the resulting project asset available with Rirei under the MIT license, to the extent
permitted by the generator's applicable terms.

The icon is intended as original Rirei artwork and should not contain third-party logos or
provider branding. Replace the asset and update this record if that provenance changes.

## Vendored Terminal Assets

The xterm.js files under `desktop/renderer/vendor/` come from the npm packages and versions
listed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. Their SHA-256 hashes match the published package files.
